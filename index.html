Four habits for better AI answers

  • Say who you are. "I'm a youth pastor at a church of 800" gets a far better answer than no context at all.
  • Give it real numbers. AI guesses when it doesn't have data. Paste the numbers instead of describing them.
  • Name the audience. A board, a volunteer team, and a lead pastor each need a different answer.
  • Ask again. If the first answer misses, reply with "shorter," "simpler," or "focus on the kids ministry." It's a conversation.
